# 🇫🇷 PEA Rules & Eligibility — Quick Reference

## What is a PEA?

The **Plan d'Épargne en Actions** (PEA) is a French tax-advantaged investment wrapper for European equities. After 5 years, capital gains and dividends are exempt from income tax (only 17.2% social charges apply).

---

## PEA Types

| Type | Max Deposit | Eligible Investments |
|------|-------------|---------------------|
| **PEA Classique** | €150,000 | EU/EEA stocks, eligible ETFs, eligible OPCVM |
| **PEA-PME** | €225,000 | Small/mid-cap EU companies meeting PME criteria |
| **PEA Jeune** | €20,000 | Same as PEA Classique (18-25, attached to parents) |

**Combined cap:** PEA + PEA-PME cannot exceed €225,000 total deposits.

---

## Eligibility Rules

### Stocks (Actions)
✅ **Eligible:** Company headquartered in EU or EEA country  
✅ Paris, Amsterdam, Brussels, Lisbon, Dublin, Oslo, Milan, Frankfurt, etc.  
❌ **NOT eligible:** UK companies (post-Brexit), Swiss companies, US companies  
❌ **NOT eligible:** REITs/SIICs (since 2012 for new positions)

### ETFs & OPCVM
✅ **Eligible if:** Contains ≥75% PEA-eligible equities  
✅ Many Amundi, Lyxor, iShares UCITS ETFs qualify  
✅ Some World/S&P 500 ETFs qualify via **synthetic replication** (swap-based)  
⚠️ Always verify "PEA" label from broker

### PEA-PME Specific
Company must meet **ALL** of:
- < 5,000 employees
- AND (Annual revenue < €1.5B OR Total assets < €2B)
- Headquartered in EU/EEA

## Tax Rules

| Event | Before 5 Years | After 5 Years |
|-------|----------------|---------------|
| Capital gains | 30% flat tax (PFU) | 17.2% social charges only |
| Dividends | 30% flat tax | 17.2% social charges only |
| Partial withdrawal | PEA **closed** | Allowed (keeps tax advantage) |
| Full withdrawal | PEA closed | PEA closed |

### Key Tax Optimization Points
1. **Never withdraw before 5 years** unless emergency
2. **Maximize deposits early** — the 5-year clock starts at opening, not at deposit
3. **Reinvest dividends** within PEA for compound tax-free growth
4. **Use PEA-PME** for small-cap EU plays (separate €225K envelope)
5. **Losses within PEA** cannot be deducted from other income

## PEA Strategy Considerations

1. **Long-term horizon** — 5+ years mandatory for tax optimization
2. **No margin/leverage** — cannot use borrowed funds within PEA
3. **No short selling** — long-only positions
4. **No fractional shares** (at most brokers)
5. **Currency risk** for non-EUR denominated positions
6. **Dividends taxed at source** for some countries (withholding tax) — check DTAs
