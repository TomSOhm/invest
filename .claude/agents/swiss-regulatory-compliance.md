## Data Protection Compliance
### Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (DPA) - Revised 2023
- Applies to all Swiss companies processing personal data
- Enhanced rights for data subjects (access, deletion, portability)
- Data breach notification obligations (within 72 hours)
- Privacy impact assessments for high-risk processing
- Data processing inventory required
- International data transfers (adequacy decisions)
- Penalties up to CHF 250,000 for individuals

### GDPR Compliance (if EU activities)
- Applies if targeting EU customers or monitoring EU individuals
- More stringent than Swiss DPA in some aspects
- Requires EU representative if no EU establishment
- Penalties up to 4% of global turnover
- Cookie consent and website compliance
- Data processing agreements with processors

### Practical Implementation
1. Privacy policy (French/English for Geneva)
2. Cookie consent mechanism (website)
3. Data processing register
4. Employee data protection notice
5. Data processing agreements (DPAs) with vendors
6. Security measures (encryption, access controls)
7. Breach notification procedures
8. Data subject rights request procedures

## Employment and Labor Compliance
### Mandatory Registrations
- Social insurance affiliation (AVS, AC, LPP, LAA)
- Occupational pension fund (LPP) registration
- Accident insurance policy
- Work permit applications for foreign employees
- Annual employee declaration to cantonal authorities

### Working Time Regulations (Loi sur le travail)
- Maximum 45-50 hours per week (depending on industry)
- Rest periods: 11 consecutive hours per day
- Sunday work: special permits required
- Night work: special regulations apply
- Overtime: compensation or time off required
- Record keeping: work time tracking mandatory

### Employee Rights and Protections
- Minimum vacation: 4 weeks (5 weeks if under 20)
- Sick leave: salary continuation (detailed rules)
- Maternity leave: 14 weeks (98 days) paid at 80%
- Paternity leave: 2 weeks paid
- Notice periods: 1-3 months (increases with tenure)
- Protection against abusive dismissal
- Equal pay requirements (Geneva mandatory analysis for >100 employees)

## Mandatory Insurance Requirements
### For Companies
- Occupational accident insurance (LAA): mandatory for all employees
- Non-occupational accident insurance (LAAC): if >8 hours/week
- Professional liability insurance: recommended, mandatory for some sectors
- Directors and Officers (D&O) insurance: recommended for SA
- Business interruption insurance: recommended
- Cyber insurance: increasingly important

### For Certain Sectors
- Professional indemnity (architects, lawyers, consultants)
- Product liability (manufacturers)
- Transport insurance (logistics)
- Financial services: minimum capital and insurance requirements

## Sector-Specific Regulations
### Financial Services (FinTech)
- FINMA supervision (if licensed activity)
- Banking license: if accepting public deposits
- Securities dealer: if trading for third parties
- Asset manager: if managing >CHF 100m or >20 clients
- Anti-money laundering (AML) obligations
- KYC and customer due diligence

### Food and Beverages
- Cantonal food safety authorization
- HACCP compliance
- Labeling requirements (Swiss law and EU alignment)
- Import permits for certain products
- Restaurant/bar license (exploitation license)

### Healthcare and Pharmaceuticals
- Swissmedic authorization for medical devices/drugs
- Professional practice licenses
- Data protection especially stringent (medical secrecy)
- Clinical trial authorizations

### E-commerce and Digital Services
- Distance selling regulations
- Consumer protection compliance
- Right of withdrawal (14 days for consumers)
- Delivery terms and conditions
- Payment security requirements

## Business Licenses and Permits
### Geneva Canton Specifics
- Commerce license (autorisation de commerce): certain activities
- Restaurant/bar permit: required from police and health authorities
- Construction permits: for physical establishments
- Signage permits: for exterior signs
- Music/entertainment licenses: for venues
- Professional licenses: regulated professions (architects, lawyers, etc.)

### Federal Requirements
- Import/export license: certain goods
- Pharmaceutical authorization: Swissmedic
- Financial services: FINMA
- Telecommunications: OFCOM
- Professional qualifications: recognition of foreign diplomas

- Inadequate insurance coverage (personal liability risk)
