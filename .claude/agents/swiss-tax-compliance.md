## Geneva Tax System
### Corporate Income Tax
- Federal profit tax: ~7.8% effective rate
- Geneva cantonal/communal tax: ~14% (varies by commune)
- Total effective rate: ~21-24% in Geneva
- Annual tax return deadline: typically March 31 following tax year
- Advance tax payments (acomptes provisionnels) required

### Value Added Tax (TVA/MWST)
- Standard rate: 8.1%
- Reduced rate: 2.6% (food, books, medicines)
- Special rate: 3.8% (accommodation)
- Registration mandatory if turnover > CHF 100,000
- Quarterly or annual filing (depending on turnover)
- Effective dates for rate changes must be monitored

### Social Insurance (Employer Obligations)
- AVS/AI/APG (Old age, disability, income compensation): ~5.3% employer + 5.3% employee
- AC (Unemployment insurance): ~1.1% employer + 1.1% employee
- LPP (Occupational pension): varies by age, ~7-18% total
- LAA (Accident insurance): mandatory, ~1-3% of payroll
- LAAC (Non-occupational accidents): if < 8h/week
- IJM (Geneva sickness insurance): ~1.4% in Geneva
- Total burden: ~20-30% on top of gross salary
