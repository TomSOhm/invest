## Geneva Business Ecosystem
### Key Sectors and Strengths
- **International Organizations**: UN, WHO, WIPO, ILO, WTO, ICRC (>40 organizations)
- **Financial Services**: Private banking, wealth management, asset management
- **Commodities Trading**: Global trading hub (oil, metals, grains)
- **Watchmaking and Luxury**: Haute horlogerie, jewelry, luxury goods
- **Life Sciences**: Biotech, pharma, medtech clusters
- **Technology**: Growing startup ecosystem, fintech, AI/ML
- **Professional Services**: Law firms, consulting, audit (big 4)

### Market Characteristics
- Small but high-value market (GDP per capita: ~CHF 85,000)
- Multilingual: French (Geneva), German, Italian, English
- High cost base: salaries, real estate, operations
- Quality-focused: premium positioning often required
- Conservative buyers: trust and relationships matter
- International orientation: think beyond Switzerland from day one
- Regulatory environment: generally business-friendly but detailed

## Market Entry Strategies
### Option 1: Direct Market Entry
**Pros**: Full control, direct customer relationships, brand building
**Cons**: High setup costs, longer time to market, regulatory burden
**Best for**: Well-funded companies, committed to Swiss market long-term

### Option 2: Partnership/Distribution
**Pros**: Faster entry, leverages existing networks, lower initial investment
**Cons**: Less control, margin sharing, dependency on partner
**Best for**: Testing market, limited resources, complex regulatory environment

### Option 3: Acquisition
**Pros**: Immediate market presence, team and customer base included
**Cons**: High cost, integration challenges, cultural fit risk
**Best for**: Established companies, strategic consolidation, talent acquisition

### Option 4: Hybrid (Light Presence + Partnerships)
**Pros**: Balanced approach, flexibility, manageable risk
**Cons**: Coordination complexity, potential channel conflicts
**Best for**: B2B SaaS, professional services, most startups

## Exit Strategy Considerations
### Swiss M&A Market
- Active but smaller than US/UK
- Strategic buyers: Swiss corporates, international players
- Private equity: growing interest in Swiss SMEs
- Typical multiples: 5-10x EBITDA (sector dependent)
- Process: 6-12 months typical transaction timeline

### IPO Options
- Swiss Exchange (SIX): High standards, costly for small companies
- Alternative markets: NASDAQ, Euronext (for international companies)
- SPACs: emerging option but limited in Europe
- Minimum requirements: consider from CHF 50-100m+ valuation

### Preparing for Exit
- Clean cap table and governance
- Strong financial reporting and controls
- Scalable operations and systems
- Customer concentration risk mitigation
- Key person dependency reduction
- IP and legal compliance
- Data room preparation
