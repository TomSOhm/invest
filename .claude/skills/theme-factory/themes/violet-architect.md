# Violet Architect

A premium dark theme with violet and fuchsia accents, embodying Swiss precision and AI innovation. Created for FREMA Consulting's brand identity.

## Color Palette

- **Void**: `#050505` - Primary background, deepest layer
- **Midnight**: `#0a0a0f` - Secondary background, navbar
- **Charcoal**: `#111118` - Card backgrounds, elevated surfaces
- **Slate**: `#18181b` - Muted surfaces, subtle backgrounds
- **Steel**: `#27272a` - Borders, dividers
- **Violet**: `#8b5cf6` - Main interactive elements, CTAs, links
- **Violet Light**: `#a78bfa` - Hover states, gradient endpoints
- **Violet Dark**: `#7c3aed` - Pressed states, emphasis
- **Fuchsia**: `#d946ef` - Secondary accent, highlights
- **Fuchsia Light**: `#e879f9` - Hover/active secondary
- **White**: `#ffffff` - Primary text, headings
- **Off-White**: `#fafafa` - Body text
- **Silver**: `#a1a1aa` - Secondary text, descriptions
- **Gray**: `#71717a` - Muted text, placeholders
- **Success**: `#34d399` - Positive indicators, growth
- **Warning**: `#fbbf24` - Caution, alerts
- **Error**: `#f87171` - Risk, danger
- **Info**: `#38bdf8` - Neutral information

## Typography

- **Headers**: Syne Bold (weights 400-800)
- **Body Text**: Inter
- **Code**: JetBrains Mono
- **Accent/Quotes**: Instrument Serif
- **Technical Metrics**: Orbitron

## Special Effects

- **Glass Morphism**: `rgba(17, 17, 24, 0.85)` + `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`
- **Gradient Accent**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #8b5cf6 0%, #a78bfa 100%)`
- **Glow Shadow**: `0 0 40px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.2)`
- **Border Default**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`

## Best Used For

AI/ML consulting, investor-facing business plans, technical proposals, premium dark-themed presentations, Swiss enterprise documents, data science reports, SaaS pitch decks.
