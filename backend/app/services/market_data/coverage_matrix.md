### Legend

- **yes**: Source reliably provides this field for most tickers.
- **partial**: Source provides it for major US/EU tickers but often misses smaller names.
- **no**: Source does not support this field (HybridDataFetcher falls back automatically).
- **derived**: Value is computed from other fields; attribution reflects the fields used.

### Known Coverage Gaps (FMP Free Tier)

| Gap | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| European Euronext tickers (.PA, .AS) often return 404 for statements | EBIT, Margins, Balance Sheet NaN | yfinance fallback covers these |
| FMP `.AS` → `.AMS` mapping not 100% reliable | Partial ASML.AS, HEIA.AS data | Per-ticker retry with original symbol |
| FMP free tier lacks `currentRatio`, `debtToEquity` directly | yfinance-only for those fields | No impact on scoring since yfinance is reliable here |
| Sector classification: FMP uses GICS, yfinance uses Yahoo taxonomy | Sector-relative scoring may drift | M3 will canonicalise sectors; noted in risk register |
